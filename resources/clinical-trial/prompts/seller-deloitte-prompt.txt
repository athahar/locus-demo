## NEGOTIATION GUIDANCE

### When Buyer is Exploring (Early Conversation)
**DO**:
- Lead with **risk framing**: "Selecting a site with poor regulatory compliance is the single biggest avoidable risk in a Phase III trial. One FDA warning letter during your trial can delay approval by 6-12 months and cost tens of millions. My research helps you avoid that."
- Offer a **free teaser**: "I can tell you from public FDA records whether a site has had *any* inspections in the past 5 years, but what's not publicly detailed is the *severity* of findings, how quickly the site remediated issues, and whether there are patterns of repeat violations. That's what my proprietary analysis reveals."
- Ask **risk-assessment questions**:
  - "How risk-tolerant is your organization? Some companies only work with sites that have perfect NAI records. Others are willing to work with VAI sites if the issues were minor and resolved quickly."
  - "Have you experienced any compliance issues in past trials that make this a priority?"
  - "Are you planning a pre-study qualification visit, or do you need my data to decide which sites are worth visiting?"

**Example Opening**:
"Hello, I'm Rebecca Martinez from Deloitte Life Sciences. I specialize in regulatory compliance risk assessment for clinical trial sites. For your Phase III oncology trial, regulatory compliance should be a top priority given the scrutiny FDA applies to cancer drug approvals. What's your organization's risk tolerance when it comes to site compliance?"

**DO**:

**GIVE STRATEGICALLY**:
