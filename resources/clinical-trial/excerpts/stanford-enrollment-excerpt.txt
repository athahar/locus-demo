METHODOLOGY:
This report analyzes ClinicalTrials.gov registry data for 8,547 Phase III
oncology trials conducted between 2019-2024. We extracted site-level enrollment
metrics for 60 major U.S. academic medical centers and NCI-designated cancer
centers. Data includes:
  1. Enrollment velocity (patients/month per site)
  2. Time to first patient enrolled
  3. Screening-to-enrollment conversion ratios
  4. Patient retention and dropout rates
  5. Cost-per-enrolled-patient estimates
  6. Seasonal enrollment patterns
  7. Site activation timelines

All metrics validated against sponsor-reported outcomes where available.

SCOPE: This Key Findings Report provides industry-wide benchmarks (aggregate
data) plus detailed performance profiles for the top 8 "Elite Performer" sites.
Full report (124 pages, $0.09) includes all 60 sites, predictive models, and
comprehensive case studies.

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY: THE ENROLLMENT CRISIS
================================================================================

INDUSTRY CHALLENGE:
-------------------
Enrollment failure is the #1 reason clinical trials do not succeed:
  • 85% of Phase III oncology trials fail to meet original enrollment timelines
  • 35% of cancer trials close early due to insufficient enrollment
  • Average enrollment delay: 6.8 months beyond target completion date
  • Estimated cost of delay: $600,000 - $1.2 million per day for late-stage
    cancer drug development

ROOT CAUSES IDENTIFIED IN OUR RESEARCH:
  1. Overestimation of site enrollment capacity (59% of trials)
  2. Overly restrictive eligibility criteria (42% of trials)
  3. Lack of site performance vetting (38% of sponsors)
  4. Poor patient recruitment strategies (33% of sites)
  5. Inadequate research coordinator staffing (28% of sites)

KEY INSIGHT: Site selection is THE most controllable variable in enrollment
success. Our research shows that choosing elite-performing sites vs. average
sites can reduce enrollment time by 40-50%.

INDUSTRY BENCHMARKS: PHASE III ONCOLOGY TRIALS (2019-2024 AGGREGATE DATA)

ENROLLMENT VELOCITY
-------------------
Metric: Average number of patients enrolled per site per month

MEDIAN PERFORMANCE (All Phase III Oncology Trials):
  • Solid Tumors: 0.8 patients/site/month (IQR: 0.4 - 1.6)
  • Hematologic Malignancies: 0.6 patients/site/month (IQR: 0.3 - 1.2)
  • Breast Cancer Specifically: 0.9 patients/site/month (IQR: 0.5 - 1.8)
  • Triple-Negative Breast Cancer: 0.7 patients/site/month (IQR: 0.3 - 1.4)
    [Lower due to rarer subtype]

PERFORMANCE TIERS:
  • Elite Performers (Top 10%): 2.5+ patients/site/month
  • Above Average (Top 25%): 1.6 - 2.4 patients/site/month
  • Average (50th percentile): 0.8 patients/site/month
  • Below Average (Bottom 25%): 0.3 - 0.6 patients/site/month
  • Chronic Underperformers (Bottom 10%): <0.3 patients/site/month

IMPLICATIONS FOR TRIAL PLANNING:
  • For a 200-patient trial over 18 months:
    - Using MEDIAN sites (0.8 pt/mo): Need 14 sites
    - Using ELITE sites (2.5 pt/mo): Need 5 sites
    - Cost savings from fewer sites: $2-4 million in site management costs

TIME TO FIRST PATIENT ENROLLED (TTFPE)
---------------------------------------
Metric: Days from site initiation visit (SIV) to first patient enrolled

MEDIAN PERFORMANCE:
  • All Phase III Oncology: 68 days (IQR: 45 - 92 days)
  • Breast Cancer Trials: 58 days (IQR: 40 - 82 days)

PERFORMANCE TIERS:
  • Elite Performers: 30-45 days (Top 10%)
  • Above Average: 46-60 days (Top 25%)
  • Average: 61-75 days (50th percentile)
  • Below Average: 76-100 days (Bottom 25%)
  • Chronic Underperformers: >100 days (Bottom 10%)

KEY FACTORS AFFECTING TTFPE:
  1. Site activation efficiency (regulatory submissions, contracts)
  2. Pre-identification of eligible patients (screening logs)
  3. Research coordinator availability
  4. Investigator engagement and prioritization

SCREENING-TO-ENROLLMENT RATIO
------------------------------
Metric: Number of patients screened to yield one enrolled patient

MEDIAN PERFORMANCE:
  • All Phase III Oncology: 3.2:1 (3.2 patients screened per 1 enrolled)
  • Breast Cancer Trials: 2.8:1
  • Triple-Negative Breast Cancer: 4.1:1 (higher due to specific biomarker)

PERFORMANCE TIERS:
  • Elite Performers: 2.0:1 or better (highly efficient screening)
  • Average: 3.0-3.5:1
  • Below Average: 5.0:1 or worse (indicates restrictive criteria or poor pre-screening)

IMPLICATIONS:
  • Sites with poor screening ratios (>5:1) may have inadequate patient
    population or inefficient screening processes
  • High screen failure rates increase site costs and timeline

PATIENT RETENTION & TRIAL COMPLETION RATES
-------------------------------------------
Metric: Percentage of enrolled patients who complete all protocol-required visits

MEDIAN PERFORMANCE:
  • All Phase III Oncology: 78% trial completion rate
  • Breast Cancer Trials: 82% trial completion rate

DROPOUT REASONS (Most Common):
  1. Adverse events / toxicity (32% of dropouts)
  2. Patient withdrawal of consent (28%)
  3. Disease progression (18%)
  4. Lost to follow-up / logistical issues (12%)
  5. Death (8%)
  6. Other (2%)

PERFORMANCE TIERS:
  • Elite Performers: >85% completion rate
  • Average: 75-82% completion rate
  • Below Average: <70% completion rate

KEY INSIGHT: Sites with strong patient support services (transportation
assistance, dedicated research nurses, flexible visit scheduling) have 12%
higher retention rates on average.

SITE ACTIVATION TIMELINE
-------------------------
Metric: Days from site selection to site initiation visit (SIV)

MEDIAN PERFORMANCE:
  • All Phase III Oncology: 142 days (IQR: 98 - 186 days)

BREAKDOWN BY PHASE:
  • Regulatory submissions (IND/IDE, institutional approvals): 35 days median
  • IRB/IEC approval: 45 days median
  • Contract negotiation and budget finalization: 38 days median
  • Site training and SIV scheduling: 24 days median

PERFORMANCE TIERS:
  • Elite Performers: <100 days total activation time
  • Average: 120-150 days
  • Below Average: >180 days

IMPLICATION: Slow site activation delays first patient enrollment and overall
trial timeline. Sites with streamlined contracting and efficient IRBs are critical.

COST PER ENROLLED PATIENT
--------------------------
Metric: Total site costs divided by number of patients enrolled
(Based on sponsor-disclosed budgets for 142 trials in our dataset)

MEDIAN COST:
  • Phase III Oncology: $42,000 per enrolled patient
  • Range: $28,000 (simple protocols) to $68,000 (complex, multi-visit protocols)

COST BREAKDOWN:
  • Site management fees: 35%
  • Per-patient visit payments: 40%
  • Investigator fees: 12%
  • Lab/imaging/procedures: 10%
  • Other (regulatory, monitoring): 3%

PERFORMANCE TIERS:
  • Cost-efficient sites: $30,000-$38,000 per patient
  • Average sites: $39,000-$48,000 per patient
  • High-cost sites: >$50,000 per patient

KEY INSIGHT: Elite enrollment sites are NOT necessarily more expensive. In
fact, faster enrollment reduces overhead costs (site management, monitoring),
often making elite sites more cost-effective overall.

SEASONAL ENROLLMENT PATTERNS
-----------------------------
Metric: Enrollment volume by month (aggregate across all trials)

FINDINGS:
  • HIGHEST enrollment months: September, October, January (post-holidays)
  • LOWEST enrollment months: July, August, December (vacations, holidays)
  • Average variability: 28% difference between peak and trough months

IMPLICATION: Trial launch timing matters. Launching site activations in Q3
(July-Sept) positions for strong Q4/Q1 enrollment. Avoid site launches in Nov-Dec.

ACTIONABLE RECOMMENDATIONS: TRIAL PLANNING & SITE SELECTION
================================================================================

STEP 1: SET REALISTIC ENROLLMENT TARGETS
-----------------------------------------
Use our benchmarks to calculate required number of sites:

FORMULA:
Number of Sites = (Target Enrollment) / (Enrollment Velocity × Enrollment Period)

EXAMPLE: 200-patient trial, 18-month enrollment
  • Using MEDIAN sites (0.8 pt/mo): 200 / (0.8 × 18) = 14 sites needed
  • Using ELITE sites (2.5 pt/mo): 200 / (2.5 × 18) = 5 sites needed

RECOMMENDATION: Always build in 20% buffer (add 1-2 backup sites) to account
  for underperformance.

STEP 2: PRIORITIZE SITE SELECTION CRITERIA
-------------------------------------------
Rank these factors based on your trial's specific needs:
  1. Enrollment velocity (critical for tight timelines)
  2. Patient retention (critical for long follow-up periods)
  3. Time to first patient (critical if competing with rival sponsors)
  4. Diversity (critical for FDA approval requirements)
  5. Cost (balance against enrollment speed - faster may save money overall)

STEP 3: CONDUCT FEASIBILITY ASSESSMENTS
----------------------------------------
For each candidate site, ask these questions during feasibility calls:
  ✓ "How many [specific cancer type/stage] patients do you see annually?"
  ✓ "How many active oncology trials are you currently enrolling?"
  ✓ "What was your average enrollment rate in your past 3 Phase III trials?"
  ✓ "How long does your IRB typically take for initial protocol approval?"
  ✓ "How many dedicated research coordinators do you have for oncology trials?"

RED FLAGS during feasibility:
  ❌ Site cannot provide enrollment metrics from past trials
  ❌ Site estimates "we can easily enroll 50+ patients" without data to support
  ❌ Site has >30 active trials (potential patient competition)
  ❌ Site coordinator turnover >50% in past 2 years

STEP 4: NEGOTIATE PERFORMANCE-BASED CONTRACTS
----------------------------------------------
Structure site budgets to incentivize enrollment:
  • 60% per-patient milestone payments (encourages enrollment)
  • 20% retention bonuses (paid when patient completes trial)
  • 15% site management/overhead fees
  • 5% early enrollment bonus (sites exceeding targets)

AVOID: Front-loading payments (site has no incentive to enroll after receiving
  upfront fees).

STEP 5: MONITOR & MANAGE PERFORMANCE
-------------------------------------
Track these metrics monthly:
  • Actual vs. projected enrollment (site-level)
  • Screen failure rates (identify eligibility issues early)
  • Dropout reasons (address logistical barriers proactively)
  • Time to resolve queries (data quality indicator)

INTERVENTION TRIGGERS:
  • If site is >2 months behind enrollment target → Schedule investigator call
  • If screen failure rate >50% → Review eligibility criteria with site
  • If dropout rate >25% → Assess patient support needs
