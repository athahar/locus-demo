REPORT METADATA

Published: January 2025
Principal Author: Rebecca Martinez, JD (Former FDA CDER Compliance Officer)
Contributing Analysts: Deloitte Regulatory Affairs Team (8 analysts)
Data Period: January 2019 - December 2024
Scope: FDA BIMO (Bioresearch Monitoring) inspection records for 45 major
       U.S. clinical research sites conducting Phase II-IV trials

METHODOLOGY:
This scorecard analyzes FDA inspection data from three sources:
  1. FDA Inspection Classification Database (public outcomes)
  2. Form 483 observations (obtained via FOIA requests)
  3. Warning Letters and Untitled Letters (public record)
  4. Site-reported CAPA (Corrective Action/Preventive Action) documentation
  5. IRB approval timeline benchmarking (proprietary survey data)

Each site receives a COMPLIANCE SCORE (0-100 scale) based on:
  • Inspection outcomes (NAI = 100 pts, VAI = 60 pts, OAI = 0 pts)
  • Remediation speed (CAPA closure time)
  • Repeat violations (penalty for same issues appearing multiple times)
  • IRB efficiency (approval timeline, protocol amendments)
  • Data integrity indicators (audit findings)

SCOPE: This Compliance Scorecard covers 15 top-tier sites. Full report
(89 pages, $0.05) includes all 45 sites with detailed Form 483 analyses,
5-year trend data, and remediation histories.

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY: KEY FINDINGS
================================================================================

COMPLIANCE LANDSCAPE OVERVIEW (2019-2024)
------------------------------------------
• FDA conducted 312 clinical investigator inspections during this period
• Overall classifications: 68% NAI, 28% VAI, 4% OAI
• Most common Form 483 observations:
  1. Informed consent documentation issues (32% of inspections)
  2. Protocol deviations not properly documented (28%)
  3. Inadequate drug accountability records (18%)
  4. Adverse event reporting delays (12%)
  5. Source document deficiencies (10%)

• Warning letters issued to clinical investigators: 8 (0.6% of sites)
• Voluntary Action Indicated (VAI) sites: Average 4.2 observations per 483

TOP-TIER SITE PERFORMANCE:
• 5 sites achieved "Gold Standard" status (5+ years, zero observations)
• 10 sites have strong compliance records (1-2 minor observations, all resolved)
• 3 sites have red flags warranting caution

TREND ANALYSIS:
• COVID-19 period (2020-2021): 40% reduction in routine inspections
• 2022-2024: FDA resumed aggressive inspection schedule
• Increased focus on data integrity and electronic source documentation

  • NAI (No Action Indicated): No objectionable conditions or practices found

DECISION FRAMEWORK: USING COMPLIANCE DATA FOR SITE SELECTION
================================================================================

RECOMMENDED APPROACH:
---------------------
1. TIER YOUR CANDIDATE SITES BY COMPLIANCE SCORE
   • Tier 1 (Score 95-100): Prioritize for site selection
   • Tier 2 (Score 85-94): Acceptable with standard oversight
   • Tier 3 (Score 75-84): Require enhanced monitoring plan
   • Below 75: Avoid or require comprehensive pre-study audit

2. EVALUATE RECENT INSPECTION TIMING
   • Site inspected within past 12 months with NAI → Lower risk
   • Site with VAI but subsequent NAI → Demonstrates good remediation
   • Site with recent VAI (within 6 months) → Defer or require CAPA review
   • Site not inspected in 5+ years → Unknown risk (could be positive or negative)

3. ASSESS TYPE OF OBSERVATIONS
   • Minor documentation issues → Lower risk, easily corrected
   • Informed consent issues → Moderate risk, requires training verification
   • SAE reporting failures → Higher risk, suggests oversight gaps
   • Data integrity concerns → Highest risk, fundamental quality issues

4. CONSIDER TRIAL-SPECIFIC RISK FACTORS
   • High-risk patient population (oncology, pediatrics) → Select only Tier 1 sites
   • Simple trial design → Tier 2 sites acceptable
   • Complex endpoints, frequent monitoring → Prioritize sites with robust infrastructure

5. DUE DILIGENCE RECOMMENDATIONS
   For Tier 2 sites (scores 85-94):
   • Request copy of most recent FDA inspection report + CAPA documentation
   • Conduct phone interview with site QA/regulatory lead
   • Review site's internal audit schedule and recent findings
   • Include compliance language in clinical trial agreement

   For Tier 3 sites (scores 75-84):
   • Require pre-study qualification audit by sponsor
   • Request investigator CV, training records, and GCP certificates
   • Escalated monitoring plan (more frequent site visits)
   • Consider backup site in same region
